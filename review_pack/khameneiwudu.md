### Path 5: `kwrooto2`

1. **Q:** What is your question about?  
   **A chosen:** I performed wuḍūʾ and am not sure whether it has become void  
   *Option rests on:* wududoubtvoid: "doubt concerning state of purity after the performance of wuḍū’"  

**Outcome:**

> **No attention should be paid to doubt concerning state of purity after the performance of wuḍū’**. And it is permissible for one to offer prayer and read the Noble Qur’an, etc. as long as one is not certain that his wuḍū’ has been invalidated.
>
> *Citation (wududoubtvoid):* Practical Laws of Islam, Q 122 · https://www.leader.ir/en/book/32/1?sn=5142
>
> *Official Urdu of the same ruling (for the Urdu reader; the helper shows the English quote):*

### Path 11: `kwrooto4-kwinvo5-kwfluido1`

1. **Q:** What is your question about?  
   **A chosen:** I want to know whether something invalidates wuḍūʾ  
   *Option rests on:* wuduinvalidators: "پیشاب نکلنا"  
2. **Q:** What happened?  
   **A chosen:** Some fluid came out (madhī, wadhī, wadī, or a doubtful fluid after urinating)  
   *Option rests on:* dischargesmadhi: "pure and do not invalidate wuḍū’"  
3. **Q:** Which describes the fluid?  
   **A chosen:** The fluid called madhī, wadhī or wadī  
   *Option rests on:* dischargesmadhi: "All of them are pure and do not invalidate wuḍū’"  

**Outcome:**

> The wetness that comes out occasionally after the discharge of semen is called ‘wadhi’ That which comes out occasionally after urinating is called ‘wadi’, and that which comes out after foreplay is called ‘madhi’. **All of them are pure and do not invalidate wuḍū’**.
>
> *Citation (dischargesmadhi):* Practical Laws of Islam, Q 92 · https://www.leader.ir/en/book/32/1?sn=5141
>
